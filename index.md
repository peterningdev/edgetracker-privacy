# EdgeTracker Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 1, 2026

EdgeTracker is an offline iOS app for recording bankrolls, poker sessions, tournament results, transactions, and related financial information.

## Information stored by the app

The app stores the information you enter on your device, including bankroll names and balances, deposits and withdrawals, cash-session and tournament records, dates and durations, currencies, notes, tags, settings, saved filters, and active live-session state. The app does not require an account.

## Collection, transmission, and sharing

EdgeTracker does not operate a server and does not collect, sell, or share your information through an EdgeTracker service. The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting, or other third-party data-collection services. The app does not send your records over the network on its own. If you choose to export or share a file, the file may be transmitted by the system or the destination app or service, which is then responsible for its own privacy practices. The privacy manifest declares app-only UserDefaults use, tracking disabled, and no collected data types.

## Backups and exports

When you choose **Export Full Backup** (a JSON backup) or a CSV export, the app creates a file on your device and presents Apple’s system file or sharing interface. You control whether and where that file is saved or shared. A file shared with another app or service is governed by that recipient’s privacy practices. Imported files are read only when you select them through the system file picker.

## Retention and deletion

Your records remain on the device until you edit or delete them. The **Settings → Clear All Data** action deletes the app’s stored sessions, bankrolls, transactions, saved filters, managed lists, and related preferences. Exported or backed-up files are separate copies; delete those files from the locations where you saved or shared them. Uninstalling the app may also remove its local container according to iOS behavior, but it does not remove copies you exported elsewhere.

## Children

EdgeTracker is not designed to collect personal information from children or from anyone else. It has no account system or online service.

For privacy questions or requests, contact [support.edgetracker@gmail.com](mailto:support.edgetracker@gmail.com).
