# EdgeTracker Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 1, 2026

EdgeTracker is an offline iOS app for recording bankrolls, poker sessions, tournament results, transactions, and related financial information.

## Information stored by the app

The app stores the information you enter on your device, including bankroll names and balances, deposits and withdrawals, cash-session and tournament records, dates and durations, currencies, notes, tags, settings, saved filters, and active live-session state. The app does not require an account.

## Collection, transmission, and sharing

EdgeTracker does not operate a server and does not collect, sell, or share your information through an EdgeTracker service. The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting, or other third-party data-collection services. The app does not send your records over the network on its own. If you choose to export or share a file, the file may be transmitted by the system or the destination app or service, which is then responsible for its own privacy practices. The privacy manifest declares app-only UserDefaults access and system uptime access to measure elapsed time between operations within the app. Tracking remains disabled, and no collected data types are declared.

## Backups and exports

Under **Manage Data → Data Transfer**, **Create Full Backup** prepares a JSON file on your device and opens Apple’s Files interface so you can choose whether and where to save it. A full backup can contain personal poker and financial records. EdgeTracker does not encrypt or password-protect the exported file; choose a location you trust.

**Export All Sessions** and **Export Filtered Sessions** save CSV reports through the system file interface. A filtered export uses your current Results selection. **Import Sessions** and **Restore Full Backup** read a file you select through the system file picker. A confirmed full restore replaces the active database and the settings covered by the backup.

## Retention and deletion

You can edit or delete individual completed sessions and bankroll transactions, and discard unfinished live sessions where the app allows it. Pending live-session recovery can temporarily prevent changes to the affected completed session. A bankroll can be deleted only if it has no linked sessions or transactions and no other protected references. **Manage Data → Data Transfer → Clear All Data** offers a permanent reset after two explicit confirmations. It clears completed sessions, bankrolls, transactions, user-managed catalogs, saved filters, and application settings. Unfinished Live sessions and unresolved recovery block reset.

Deleting a record removes it from the active database. It does not automatically remove copies you previously saved, exported, or shared. Delete those files separately from the locations where you stored them. To protect against interrupted restores, EdgeTracker can also retain a prior database and a private rescue backup on the device. Deleting an active record does not automatically remove it from those internal recovery copies. Clear All Data retires verified app-owned recovery copies through resumable cleanup. If cleanup cannot complete safely, the app retains recovery evidence and does not report a completed reset. Technical installation identity, reset retirement markers, and empty storage remnants may remain for safe operation. Previously exported Files or iCloud Drive backups must be deleted separately. EdgeTracker does not claim forensic erasure of physical iOS storage. Uninstalling the app may remove its local container according to iOS behavior, but it does not remove copies you exported elsewhere.

## Children

EdgeTracker is not designed to collect personal information from children or from anyone else. It has no account system or online service.
